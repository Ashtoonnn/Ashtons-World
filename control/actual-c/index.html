Quilting for Queers

Welcome to quilting for queers. A small publication focused on all things quilting from a queer perspective. This project was made for my core III class and then transferred to a digital form for easier accessibility. I wanted to showcase an alternative, yet structured, piece that's informative for those interested in the origins and practices of quilting. I wanted to ensure that this piece is easily readable and easy to navigate for those who are curious. Thank you so much for coming to my page and I hope you enjoy it :).
